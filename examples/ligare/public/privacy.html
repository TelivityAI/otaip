Privacy Policy — Ligare

Ligare is a sandbox demonstration by Telivity, built on OTAIP. Last updated: 2026.

What this is

Ligare is a demo that connects travel inventory to ChatGPT. It runs against Duffel's test (sandbox) environment. Flights, prices, and bookings are simulated and not real.

Data we receive

When you search or create a test booking through the assistant, we receive the trip details you provide (such as origin, destination, dates, and any passenger details you enter) solely to forward them to the Duffel test API and return results. If you submit your email on our landing page, we use it only to contact you about connecting your own inventory.

Usage data we log

To measure and improve the product, we log non-identifying usage events for each request: the tool used (search/price/book), the structured search parameters (such as route, dates, cabin, and passenger counts), result counts, outcomes, and timing. We do not log passenger names, dates of birth, passport numbers, contact details, or payment data.

Data we do not collect

We do not sell your data. We do not store payment card details. Because this is a sandbox, do not enter real passport numbers, payment information, or other sensitive personal data.

Third parties

Search and booking requests are sent to Duffel (duffel.com) in test mode. Conversations occur within ChatGPT, governed by OpenAI's policies.
